### 3. 🤖 Lead Automation System

**Tools:** n8n, Airtable, Google Gemini, Gmail, Twilio

Built an automated lead-management workflow that captures leads, scores them using AI, categorizes them, and triggers personalized follow-ups.

**Automation workflow:**

```text
Lead Form
    ↓
Lead Capture
    ↓
Airtable CRM
    ↓
AI Lead Scoring
    ↓
Hot / Warm / Cold
    ↓
Personalized Follow-up
    ↓
Email / WhatsApp Alert
    ↓
Daily Marketing Report
```

**Key features:**

* Automated lead capture
* AI-powered lead scoring
* Lead segmentation
* Personalized email follow-ups
* Hot-lead alerts
* Automated reporting
* CRM workflow automation
